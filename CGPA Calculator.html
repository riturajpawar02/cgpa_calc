Grade points used (10-point scale, the one most Indian universities follow):

SGPA = sum of (credits × grade points) ÷ total credits. CGPA is the same sum across every semester. The percentage uses CGPA × 9.5, which is common but not universal, so check your university’s own formula before putting it on a form.
